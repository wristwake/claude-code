Captions · readable without sound

Animated captions

Style spec

  • Font: Montserrat ExtraBold, or Poppins Bold if Montserrat isn't in your CapCut. Sentence case; highlighted keywords in CAPS. Both fonts include ä, ö, ü and ß. Check "SPÜREN" renders correctly.
  • Size: cap height about 55–60 px on the 1080-wide canvas. A 4-word line should span about 60–75% of the width.
  • Color: white text, 3–4 px black stroke at about 70% opacity plus a soft shadow. Highlight color #FFC24A (amber, like sunrise).
  • Position: center at y ≈ 1400 px (73% from the top) on every shot. This stays above the TikTok and Reels bottom UI, clears every display close-up, and sits in the end card's clear lower third. Keep text within x 140–940 px, clear of the right-hand button column.
  • Grouping: 1–5 words per caption, one line preferred, never more than two. German words run longer: "HANDGELENK" alone on a line is fine.
  • Animation: words pop in at 90%→100% scale with opacity 0→1 over about 4 frames. Highlighted words get a 108% scale "punch". No bouncing and no per-letter effects.

Project setup

  • New project → ratio 9:16; set resolution 1080p and 30 fps at export.
  • Build in this order: VO first, then picture cut to the VO, then SFX, music, captions, and grading last.
  • Kling clips: use "Canvas fill" so they fill the 9:16 frame (no black bars). Zoom them by 102% to hide edge warping.

Color

  • Shots 1–4: cool. Temp −10, saturation −10, slight fade. Keep faces natural.
  • Shot 7: warm. Temp +10, highlights −5.
  • Product clips: no filter. Your renders are the reference for product color; never tint the band.
  • Add light grain (about 10) to the AI shots only, which hides the AI smoothness.
Audio mix & export

Mix, export and pre-launch check

Mix

  • The voice is king: normalize it so the loudest words sit around −6 dBFS. Use CapCut's noise reduction only if you hear hiss.
  • Ducking: put volume keyframes on the music about 0.2 s before each VO line (down) and 0.2 s after it (up). If your CapCut shows an auto-ducking option, use that instead.
  • No sudden jumps: give every SFX and music edit at least a 2–3 frame fade, except the deliberate alarm cut at 2.9 s.
  • Target about −14 LUFS integrated with true peak at −1 dBTP or lower. Platforms normalize differently, so treat this as a sensible reference, not a rule. CapCut on iPad has no loudness meter. To measure, open the export on a computer with the free Youlean Loudness Meter. Otherwise check by ear on an iPhone speaker at 50% volume and on earbuds: no clipping, and every word clear.

Export

  • 1080p · 30 fps · bitrate "Higher" (or about 12–16 Mbps) · H.264 · SDR (HDR off).
  • Export one master file per variant and language: WW_B_EN_v1.mp4, WW_B_DE_v1.mp4, and so on.
  • Watch the export on your phone in the TikTok and Instagram upload preview (don't post). Check that captions clear the UI, the first frame isn't black, and the audio starts at frame 0.

Pre-launch checklist

  • Every product claim is on the verified list; no charge time or standby claim anywhere
  • No sound plays over a product shot that could read as the band beeping
  • No AI frame shows a fake band or watch
  • Disclosure text visible on AI shots
  • Music and SFX license screenshots saved
  • Watched once muted: the story still reads
  • wristwake.shop opens the product page on mobile and checkout works

Paid test

A conservative test plan

Step 0 · before spending (free)

  • Price at €34.99 (or at least €29.99)
  • Product page: a 3-step "How it works", "silent alarm, vibration only", "charges via built-in USB-A, no dock" with a photo of your own unit charging, how the alarm is set, delivery time, returns, FAQ, the 5 color photos and the ad video. Leave out battery figures until you've measured them.
  • Meta pixel plus Conversions API through Shopify's "Facebook & Instagram" app with data sharing set to Maximum. Then place a test purchase and confirm it in Events Manager.
  • UTM tags on every ad, so Shopify's own reports can confirm Meta's numbers
  • Post all 3 variants organically on TikTok and Instagram. Free retention data: compare average watch time and 3-second retention per hook.

Step 1 · Meta only, about €90 cap

  • Why not TikTok Promote: it optimizes for views and followers, not purchases. That probably explains why the €76 you spent there brought no sales.
  • Sales campaign, purchase conversion goal, Germany, ages 22–45, broad targeting with no interests, Advantage+ placements limited to Reels and Stories.
  • 3 ad sets × 1 ad (B, A, C) × €7/day × 4 days ≈ €84.
  • With this budget Meta won't leave its learning phase. Treat it as a creative test that finds the best hook and click. It won't prove profitability.

Stop an ad when

  • Its spend reaches 1× break-even CPA with 0 add-to-carts
  • Its spend reaches 2× break-even CPA with 0 purchases
  • Hook rate is under ~20% after €10 (re-cut it rather than kill the angle)

Continue when

  • An ad buys at or below break-even CPA. Keep it at the same budget for 7 more days.
  • Scale only after 3–5 purchases at a profitable CPA, raising the budget 20–30% every 3–4 days
  • Never scale on views, likes or clicks alone
Compliance

Truth and disclosure

  • No testimonials, reviews, star ratings or "customers say". Leo and Mia are presented as a dramatization, never as customers.
  • No promise that WristWake gets anyone up on time. No sleep or health benefits. No unverified specs: charge time, standby time, waterproofing, materials, the "soft" strap.
  • The screen icons (runner, footsteps, flame) suggest step and calorie functions. Don't mention them until the supplier confirms them.
  • On-screen "Dramatization · AI-generated scenes" during the AI shots. On TikTok, switch on the AI-generated content label. On Meta, use the AI disclosure where Ads Manager offers it. EU AI Act transparency duties for realistic synthetic video started applying in August 2026. Disclosure keeps you on the safe side; this isn't legal advice.
  • Keep license proof for the music, SFX, voice plan and video plan.
