# WristWake ad production kit (v1, no Blender)

The full plan is in `blueprint.html`, which is also published as a Claude artifact.

- `clips/`: product motion clips (1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, no audio), built only from the five official product images.
- `plates/`: original images (`src_*.png`) and clean 9:16 crops (`plate_9x16_*.png`).
- `subtitles/`: SRT plus word-timing CSV for variants B (lead), A and C. Timings are estimates; re-sync them to the final voiceover.

The arcs in `02_vibration-pulse_*` are a stylized graphic, not footage of the device.
